How this works

Twice a week each card's public page is opened in a browser, exactly as a customer would see it. The welcome offer is read into four facts — bonus, spend required, time to earn, annual fee — and compared with the last scan field by field, so a change is caught even when the page's wording stays the same. A screenshot is kept whenever something moves.

What it will not do

It never guesses. If a page cannot be read with confidence it is listed under Needs a look, not reported as changed or unchanged. Sites that disallow automated access in robots.txt are not fetched.
