How this works

Twice a week each card's public page is opened in a browser, as a customer would see it. The welcome offer is read into four facts, which are the bonus, the spend required, the time to earn it and the annual fee. They're compared with the last scan one at a time, so a change is caught even when the wording around it stays the same. A screenshot is kept whenever something moves.

Pages it can't read

If a page can't be read with confidence it goes under Needs a look and isn't reported as changed or unchanged. Sites that disallow automated access in robots.txt aren't fetched.
